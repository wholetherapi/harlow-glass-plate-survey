# Harlow Glass Plate Survey — capture log

Camera: Phase One IQ4 150MP with a 120mm macro lens.
Lighting: two softboxes at 45 degrees, polarising filters on lens and lights.
Resolution: 600 ppi at plate size; 16-bit TIFF, Adobe RGB.

## Handling notes

- Plates were supported on a custom cradle with adjustable edge rests.
- Emulsion side was oriented away from the light source to avoid glare bands.
- Cracked plates (HGP-0008 and eleven others) were captured with raking light.
- Every plate was re-imaged at f/11 and f/16; the sharper frame is the master.

Studio sessions ran from 2026-03-02 to 2026-07-18.
